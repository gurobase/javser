JAVser is a free Android app that lets you watch all your favorite JAV on your desktop and android devices without ads.

## Features
- Browse and search JAV content
- Watch videos without advertisements


JAVser is powered by a custom API.


You can download JAVser by navigating to the **releases** or by clicking [here](https://javser.com/)
